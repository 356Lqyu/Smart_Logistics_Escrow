Decentralized Escrow & Milestone-Based Logistics

Secure global trade and supply chain transactions with self-enforcing Ethereum smart contracts, automated milestone distributions, and trustless escrow protection.

Why LogisticsEscrow?

Built on Ethereum, designed for modern logistics operations that demand trust,
automation, and verifiable records.

Smart Contract Escrow

Shippers deposit full escrow amounts into secure Ethereum smart contracts. Funds remain locked on-chain until verified conditions are fulfilled.

Milestone Checkpoints

Configurable payment distribution milestones (e.g., 30% pickup, 30% warehouse arrival, 40% final delivery) ensure orderly checkpoint tracking.

Automated Refund Policy

If the delivery deadline is breached before milestones are met, the smart contract automatically issues escrow refunds back to the shipper.

ERC-20 Reputation Rewards

Earn LogiTrust tokens for on-time deliveries. Build your verifiable on-chain reputation as a carrier.

How It Works

A seamless execution lifecycle powered by Ethereum smart contracts.

01

Create & Fund Agreement

Shippers define shipment specifications, delivery priority, deadlines, and lock the required ETH amount directly into escrow.

02

Carrier Acceptance

Registered carriers browse available funded agreements, accept assignments, and change the contract status to "In Progress".

03

Milestone & Payout

Carriers submit checkpoints. Shippers review and verify each completed phase, triggering instant on-chain ETH payouts.
